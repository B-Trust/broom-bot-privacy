# Privacy Policy for BROOM TEAM Bot

**Effective Date:** October 13, 2026

Welcome to **BROOM TEAM Bot** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data and information processed through our Discord bot.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
When you use our Bot on Discord, we collect and process the following limited information required for system accounting and operation:

* **Discord User Identifiers:** Your User ID, Username, and Avatar (to associate transactions with specific operators/buyers and assign staff ranks).
* **Channel Identifiers:** Discord Channel IDs (used temporarily to manage cart states per support ticket).
* **Transaction Data:** Data entered via commands (e.g., item quantities, prices, client nicknames provided during deal registration).
* **Security & Authentication Hashes:** An MD5 hash composed of transaction parameters to prevent duplicate entries in our ledger.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:
* **Accounting & Payroll:** Calculating gross margins, commissions, and staff payroll based on rank (Junior, Middle, Senior).
* **Ledger Management:** Logging authorized transaction records to our external Google Sheets database.
* **Fraud Prevention:** Ensuring uniqueness of closed receipts and protecting against duplicate entries.
* **Memory Management:** Temporary in-memory cart data is cleared periodically (every Thursday at 00:00 UTC).

---

### 3. Data Storage & Third-Party Sharing
* **Google Sheets API:** Transaction details and user IDs/usernames are synced with our secure Google Sheets database via OAuth 2.0.
* **Data Sharing:** We **do not** sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties outside of the essential service providers (Discord and Google Cloud Services) necessary to operate the Bot.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion
* Temporary cart data is cleared automatically on a weekly basis.
* Transaction logs stored in Google Sheets are retained for internal accounting purposes.
* Users can request the removal of their personal data or account associations by contacting the Bot administrator.
